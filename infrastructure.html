World-Class Manufacturing Infrastructure

Three integrated steel plants, NABL-accredited laboratories, and renewable energy systems — built for capacity, quality, and sustainability.

Machinery & Equipment

State-of-the-Art Technology

Every machine in our plants is sourced from globally reputed OEMs and maintained to the highest operational standards.

Electric Arc Furnaces (EAF)

Two 30-tonne UHP EAFs from SMS Group Germany with Consteel® continuous scrap feeding system for energy-efficient primary steelmaking.

Ladle Refining Furnace (LRF)

Twin-position LRF for secondary metallurgy — precise temperature and chemistry control to produce low-sulphur, clean steel.

Continuous Casting Machine

Six-strand, twin-curve CCM from Danieli Italy with electromagnetic stirring for billet quality from 100×100mm to 160×160mm.

18-Stand Rolling Mill

High-speed 18-stand continuous rolling mill with flying shear, quenching box (QST), and self-tempering zone for superior TMT bar production.

Spectrometer & UTM Lab

Optical Emission Spectrometer (OES) for chemical analysis, Universal Testing Machine (UTM) for mechanical testing — all NABL certified.

Dust Extraction & ZLD

Bag filter systems (99.9% efficiency), closed-loop water recycling (ZLD), and fume extraction maintaining all CPCB ambient standards.

Quality Assurance

Our Quality Control Process

A 5-stage quality control protocol ensures every batch shipped to you meets the highest BIS and international standards.

Raw Material Testing

All incoming scrap, pig iron, and ferro-alloys are spectrometrically tested before charging to the furnace.

In-Process Control

Heat-by-heat chemistry verification at EAF and LRF stages. Temperature logging every 60 seconds during casting.

Dimensional Inspection

Automated online gauging on rolling mill exit for diameter, ovality and rib geometry per BIS IS 1786 standards.

Mechanical Testing

Bend-rebend, tensile, yield, and Charpy impact tests on every heat. NABL-certified lab with daily calibration.

MTC Issuance

Mill Test Certificate with complete chemical and mechanical data issued digitally with every consignment.

Certifications

Our Accreditations & Certifications

Internationally recognised quality and environmental certifications underpinning our commitment to excellence.

ISO 9001:2015

Quality Management System certified by Bureau Veritas. Scope covers design, manufacturing, testing, and dispatch of steel products.

BIS IS 1786 Mark

Bureau of Indian Standards licence for TMT Fe 500D and Fe 550D rebars. Licence No. CM/L-5648211. Valid through 2027.

ISO 14001:2015

Environmental Management System certification for all three plants. Zero liquid discharge achieved; on track for carbon-neutral by 2035.

ISO 45001:2018 (OHSAS)

Occupational Health & Safety Management System. LTIFR (Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate) maintained below 0.4 across all plants.

NABL Accreditation

National Accreditation Board for Testing & Calibration Laboratories. Lab Code TC-5632. Covers chemical, mechanical, and non-destructive testing.

BSE Listed (SRUSTLS)

Listed on Bombay Stock Exchange since 2007. ISIN: INE000SRUSTLS. SEBI compliant disclosures. Audited by Big 4 firm annually.
