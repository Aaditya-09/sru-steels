Our Story

Three Decades of Trusted Steel Trading

SRU Steels Limited was incorporated on 11th September 1995 by a group of visionary entrepreneurs in New Delhi. What began as a modest steel trading firm in Malviya Nagar has grown into a BSE-listed steel trading company with an authorised capital of ₹62.50 Crores and paid-up capital of ₹59.93 Crores.

Today, SRU Steels sources and supplies premium TMT bars, steel billets, wire rods, structural steel, and agri-grade steel products to the country's leading builders, contractors, agricultural businesses, and re-rolling mills across 22 states.

Guided by the principles of quality, transparency, and customer-centricity, we have forged lasting partnerships with over 1,200 clients — making us one of the most reliable names in India's steel trading sector.

Sector Focus

Cultivating the Future.

From irrigation infrastructure to cold storage and farm equipment — SRU Steels is the trusted steel partner for India's agricultural backbone.

India's agriculture sector is undergoing a massive infrastructure transformation — from modern irrigation networks and grain storage silos to mechanised farm equipment and rural connectivity. SRU Steels has been at the forefront of this transformation, supplying certified steel products specifically suited to the demands of agri-infrastructure since 2022.

Our dedicated Agri-Steel Division works directly with irrigation departments, agro-processing companies, cooperative societies, and cold chain developers across Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, and Maharashtra — ensuring timely supply of the right grade at competitive prices.
