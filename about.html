Our Story

From Trading Firm to Manufacturing Powerhouse

SRU Steels Limited was incorporated on 11th September 1995 by a group of visionary industrialists in New Delhi. What began as a modest steel trading enterprise in Malviya Nagar has evolved into a BSE-listed, integrated steel manufacturer with an authorised capital of ₹62.50 Crores and paid-up capital of ₹59.93 Crores.

Today, SRU Steels operates three state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities across Northern India, supplying premium TMT bars, steel billets, wire rods, and structural steel to the country's leading builders, contractors, and re-rolling mills across 22 states.

Guided by the principles of quality, transparency, and customer-centricity, we have forged lasting partnerships with over 1,200 clients — making us one of the most reliable names in India's steel sector.

Our Journey

Three Decades of Milestones

1995
Company Founded
SRU Steels Limited incorporated in New Delhi with initial capital of ₹5 Crores as a steel trading and distribution company.
2001
First Manufacturing Plant
Commissioned first rolling mill at Kundli, Haryana with 40,000 TPA capacity. Shifted from trading to manufacturing.
2007
BSE Listing
Listed on Bombay Stock Exchange under scrip code SRUSTLS. Raised ₹18 Cr via public issue to fund capacity expansion.
2012
BIS Certification & ISO 9001
Obtained BIS IS 1786 mark for TMT bars and ISO 9001:2008 certification — establishing quality as a core differentiator.
2016
Second Plant Commissioned
Greenfield plant at Neemrana, Rajasthan with 80,000 TPA capacity and Electric Arc Furnace for primary steel production.
2019
Authorised Capital Enhanced
Authorised capital enhanced to ₹62.50 Crores. Reached ₹524 Cr revenue milestone. Entered wire rod and HR coil segments.
2022
NABL Lab & Solar Energy
Commissioned NABL-accredited metallurgical laboratory and 2 MW captive solar power plant as part of green manufacturing drive.
2025
₹842 Cr Revenue & Rajasthan Expansion
Achieved record ₹842 Cr revenue in FY25. Board approved new 1.5 lakh TPA greenfield plant in Bhiwadi, Rajasthan.
