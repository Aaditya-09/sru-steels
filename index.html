About SRU Steels

Forging India's Future with Precision Steel

Founded in 1995, SRU Steels Limited began as a steel trading firm in New Delhi and has grown into a BSE-listed steel trading company.

Operating three strategic distribution hubs across Northern India, we supply premium TMT bars, billets, wire rods, structural steel, and agri-grade products to India's leading builders, industrialists, and agribusinesses.
